# Brainstorming & Ideation

## 1. Project Title

**PocketSmart AI – An AI-Powered Personal Budget Planning and Recommendation Assistant**

## 2. Introduction

PocketSmart AI is an intelligent budgeting and recommendation application designed to help users plan their expenses, manage budgets, and discover suitable products and services according to their needs and financial limits.

The application uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) to generate personalized recommendations for home interiors, parties, and jewelry planning. It also provides a fallback recommendation system so that users can still receive suggestions when the AI service is unavailable.

## 3. Problem Statement

People often find it difficult to plan expenses, compare available options, and make suitable purchasing decisions within a fixed budget. Manual research takes time, and users may struggle to identify choices that match their preferences and financial limitations.

PocketSmart AI aims to simplify this process through budget-based planning and personalized recommendations in a single application.

## 4. Proposed Solution

The proposed system provides an AI-powered platform where users can:

* Plan home interior expenses according to their budget.
* Calculate and organize party expenses.
* Explore jewelry recommendations based on their budget and preferences.
* Analyze an outfit image for jewelry suggestions, where supported.
* Register, log in, and access personalized application features.
* View their previous recommendations through history.
* Receive recommendations using AI or a local fallback engine.
* Access product and service source links for further information.

## 5. Brainstorming Ideas

### Idea 1: Smart Home Interior Planner

Suggest suitable furniture, decorations, and interior options based on the user's available budget.

### Idea 2: Party Budget Planner

Help users organize party expenses and explore suitable services and arrangements within their budget.

### Idea 3: AI Jewelry Recommendation

Provide jewelry suggestions based on the user's budget, preferences, and optional outfit image analysis.

### Idea 4: Personalized Recommendation History

Allow users to revisit previously generated recommendations instead of starting their planning process again.

### Idea 5: Intelligent Budget Assistance

Use AI-generated suggestions and predefined fallback recommendations to make the application more useful and reliable.

## 6. Target Users

* Students planning events with limited budgets.
* Individuals organizing parties and celebrations.
* People planning home interiors.
* Users looking for jewelry recommendations.
* Individuals who want a simpler way to organize spending decisions.

## 7. Technologies Considered

* **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Jinja2 templates.
* **Backend:** Python and FastAPI.
* **Database:** SQLite with SQLAlchemy.
* **Artificial Intelligence:** Google Gemini API.
* **Data Validation:** Pydantic.
* **Testing:** Pytest and HTTPX.

## 8. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is a user-friendly web application that helps people plan expenses and obtain personalized recommendations efficiently. PocketSmart AI aims to make budget planning easier, reduce manual research, and support informed decisions.

## 9. Conclusion

The brainstorming phase identified the need for a unified, intelligent budget planning platform. The selected concept combines budget management, AI-powered recommendations, user authentication, and recommendation history to create a practical solution for everyday planning.

The ideas developed in this phase form the foundation for the requirement analysis, system design, development, and testing phases of PocketSmart AI.
